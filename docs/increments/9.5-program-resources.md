## Assumptions (FEAT-RES-n)
- FEAT-RES-1 (A58): Pools, roles and resources belong to one program. A role belongs to a pool, a resource to a role. There is no shared rate card; the same crew in two programs is entered twice.
- FEAT-RES-2 (A58): A resource is a name, a role, a capacity (hours per working day, 0.01 to 24) and an optional availability window. It is not a user, carries no login and is never matched to the directory; naming a resource grants no access.
- FEAT-RES-3 (A58): Every row carries markings. A new row's default is the markings of its parent (pool: the program's; role: its pool's; resource: its role's), an explicit marking must cover the program's and the parent's.
  An assignment takes the markings of the task or of the resource, whichever covers the other; two incomparable markings refuse the assignment.
- FEAT-RES-4 (A58): A reader sees only rows they may read. There are no placeholders and no counts of hidden rows; hours, over-allocation, cost and banner are computed over readable rows. Names are unique per program across all rows, so a name clash is reported even
  when the clashing row is hidden (the unique index would say the same).
- FEAT-RES-5 (A58): Assignments go to tasks only (phases roll up, milestones have no duration), to open tasks only, and to active resources. Removing an assignment keeps the row (marked removed) for the audit trail.
- FEAT-RES-6 (A58): Resource changes follow the program's rule for closed programs: a Complete or Cancelled program takes none.
- FEAT-RES-7 (A58): Creating or editing a resource, role, pool or assignment never changes who can read the program or any row.
- FEAT-RES-8 (A58): The resource audit events carry keys, hours and counts, never names or text; they are normal activity feed events (hours only).
- FEAT-RES-9 (A59): Allocation counts Monday to Friday. There are no holiday or shift calendars (FEAT-PRG-3). Weeks start on Monday.
- FEAT-RES-10 (A59): An assignment is hours per day (0.01 to 24) on every working day from its start to its end (at most ten years). A varying profile is several assignments of one resource; the sums of overlapping assignments are the day's load.
- FEAT-RES-11 (A59): A resource is over-allocated on a working day when its load exceeds its capacity; outside its availability window the capacity is zero. A run of consecutive working days (across weekends) with over-allocation is one period, with the peak load and the hours above capacity.
- FEAT-RES-12 (A59): Over-allocation is judged inside one program over readable assignments. Resources of different programs are never combined, even when they are the same person.
- FEAT-RES-13 (A59): The load picture covers at most 130 weeks from the first week shown; the default window is the first to the last assignment day.
- FEAT-RES-14 (A59): Labor estimate to complete: for every assignment of an open task, the assigned hours times the share not yet done (100 minus percent complete) spread evenly over its working days after the status date, or on the next working day when none is left; none for a complete or cancelled task.
  Planned is the full loading at the rate in force each day. Hours with no rate are counted as unpriced (shown, never priced as zero silently). The status date is a parameter (default today).
- FEAT-RES-15 (A60): Money needs the role `plm-program-cost` (granted by the identity provider) in addition to the read policy on the rows. Without it: no rates in the catalog, no Cost tab, the cost view, budget, actuals, material lines and rate changes answer 403 `cost.role_required` (audited as a denial), and exports contain hours only.
  Administrators and auditors do not hold it by default. Changing money also needs Modify on the markings. A program the caller cannot read answers 404 first.
- FEAT-RES-16 (A60): One currency, USD. Nothing is converted; price-list entries in another currency are not used and are reported as such.
- FEAT-RES-17 (A60): A rate is a row "from this day, this hourly rate" (0 to 100000, two decimals). Rows are never changed; a rate for an already used start day is refused. A role created without a rate has no cost until one is set.
- FEAT-RES-18 (A60): The budget is one amount per program, cost type (Labor, Material, Other) and month; any day of the month names it. Each change is written to `prg.budget_log`; setting the same amount is refused.
- FEAT-RES-19 (A60): Actual costs: labor is hours and a resource, priced at the rate of the resource's role on the day (refused with no rate); material and other are an amount other than zero (negative is a credit). Entries are immutable. A reversal books the opposite amount of one entry on the date and with the markings of that entry (once; a reversal cannot be reversed). Actual costs, and their reversals, are accepted on a Complete or Cancelled program (final invoices); every other change of the cost plan and of resources is refused there.
- FEAT-RES-20 (A60): Material lines hold a part number, a quantity and a needed date, never a price. The estimate is made at read time from the latest released structure (leaf parts, extended quantities times the line quantity) and the sourcing price lists as the reader may read them.
- FEAT-RES-21 (A60): Price choice: active and effective entries in USD with price breaks; preferred before approved, never alternate or disapproved; the cheapest unit price of the better group at the highest break not above the needed quantity, or the first break when below it (said in the line's basis). A part without a usable price, without a released revision or without a readable price list is listed as unpriced.
- FEAT-RES-22 (A60): Structure lines the reader may not read are counted as restricted and are in no total; the markings of every part that went into an estimate are in the page banner.
- FEAT-RES-23 (A60): Material estimate to complete is the estimate of lines needed after the status date; lines needed earlier are assumed to be in the recorded actuals. Other costs have a budget and actuals only (no estimate).
- FEAT-RES-24 (A60): Money actions (rate set, budget set, cost recorded, material line added or removed) are Hidden from the activity feed and audited with keys and counts only; the reads (resources, load, cost, export) are views.

## Not built / not verified
- Timesheets or ERP feeds of actual costs (actuals are entered by hand), multiple currencies, holiday and shift calendars, resource leveling or automatic reassignment, earned value indices (CPI, SPI), per-person availability from the directory, a portfolio view across programs, editing a rate in place.
- The over-allocation of one person across programs, by design.
- Price lists with quantities in another unit than the BOM line's unit are not converted.
