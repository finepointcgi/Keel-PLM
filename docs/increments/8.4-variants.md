## Assumptions
- **FEAT-VAR-1** A product is an Assembly part; its option model is governed by that part's markings (read and modify). No separate markings.
- **FEAT-VAR-2** One value per option family; required or optional families; keys are `[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_.-]{0,39}`, case-sensitive, reserved words and/or/not/in excluded.
- **FEAT-VAR-3** A line condition names option keys; it is not tied to one product. The editor validates names against a product chosen (default: the part itself); unknown names
  evaluate as "not selected" at resolve time.
- **FEAT-VAR-4** Resolving without a variant is the full 150% structure; a variant (or custom valid selection) filters lines. Draft variants resolve (preview); baselines refuse a variant.
- **FEAT-VAR-5** Option-based alternatives use distinct find numbers; same-find alternates still need disjoint effectivity. Reference designators stay unique per parent regardless of conditions.
- **FEAT-VAR-6** MBOM lines inherit conditions from linked EBOM lines (used when any linked line is used); lines without links always apply. MBOM generation needs no change.
- **FEAT-VAR-7** A released variant is frozen; the model cannot change in a way that invalidates one (rule, retire, delete, new required option) until it is retired. Deleting options/values/variants
  in use is refused. Option use by BOM line conditions is NOT checked on delete (that would disclose hidden lines); resolve treats a missing option as not selected.
- **FEAT-VAR-8** Releasing a variant needs Modify on the product and a valid configuration; no e-signature (like other configuration/markings changes). The part release and its signatures are unchanged.
- **FEAT-VAR-9** "Which variants use this part" covers products up to 25 readable models that contain the part at Latest or Latest released; hidden lines are never traversed.
- **FEAT-VAR-10** Enumeration limit 250,000 nodes; limits: 40 options, 60 values each, 200 rules, 200 variants per product; condition at most 500 characters, 64 nodes, depth 8.
- **FEAT-VAR-11** `variant.released` is in the default subscription list of sinks (additive); no notification fan-out kind was added.
