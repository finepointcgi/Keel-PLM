## Assumptions (FEAT-CMP-n)
- FEAT-CMP-1: Substances, materials and lists are public reference data without markings, readable by every internal role, changed by administrators. Declarations have no markings and are read through the part.
- FEAT-CMP-2: A declared percent is mass percent of the declared item; each line is its own homogeneous material (no summing across lines). Mass lines need the item mass.
- FEAT-CMP-3: Exemption codes belong to a list version; a code another list does not have is "not in this version" there (RoHS 7(a) does not excuse lead under REACH).
- FEAT-CMP-4: A purchased part is a leaf of the structure; assemblies are not required to be declared. Several current declarations of a part (alternate sources) are judged by the worst. A declaration covers a line when unscoped or its revision letter equals the line's; an MBOM line (no revision) matches unscoped declarations only.
- FEAT-CMP-5: Standings: compliant, with exemption, non-compliant, conflict status unknown, no declaration, declaration expired. Assembly: non-compliant if any line is; incomplete if any gap, expired or unknown; else compliant.
- FEAT-CMP-6: Declaration status is stored with one precedence: withdrawn, expired, non-compliant, verified, declared. Verification needs the approver role and is cleared by any edit. Any non-compliant regulation (including SVHC above threshold, meaning reportable) makes it non-compliant.
- FEAT-CMP-7: The version in force is the newest published version whose effective date has come; a version can be pinned in reports and a date chosen. Publication re-evaluates all declarations; the daily job re-evaluates when an effective date passes.
- FEAT-CMP-8: A list version is owned (for the Modify rule of the review engine) by the organization of the administrator who prepared it; only that organization can submit it.
- FEAT-CMP-9: Supplier and manufacturer part are opaque text references (Sourcing is built in parallel); they are never resolved or shown with names. A supplier-provided declaration is entered by an internal author and notifies the part's watchers.
- FEAT-CMP-10: Supporting documents are links by number to the document module, checked for readability at every view; an unreadable one shows as a placeholder without its number.
- FEAT-CMP-11: Roll-ups cover only lines the reader can read; hidden parts are in no count, status or cause. Audit details and notifications hold declaration numbers, codes and counts only (no names, notes, reasons or references).
- FEAT-CMP-12: The XML export is IPC-1752-like, not conformant; the CSV is conflict-minerals shaped without smelter data.
- FEAT-CMP-13: Regulatory lists are not an awareness kind (no watch or comments); their review shows on the version page and in the workflow pages.
