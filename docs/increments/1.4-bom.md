### 1.4 Multi-level BOM (2026-10-27)
Design record: [ADR-0017](../adr/0017-bom-structure-and-explosion.md).

**Delivered**
- Migration `0005_bom.sql`: `plm.bom_usage` (immutable rows of a parent iteration), `plm.bom_working_usage` (private working copy, cascades with the check-out), guards (Assembly-only parents, no self-reference, locked revisions, EA constraints), covering indexes, grants.
- `Keel.Core.IPartIterationParticipant` (+ `IterationVetoException`): `PartService` calls registered participants in its check-out, check-in and undo transactions. `Keel.Bom.BomParticipant` copies structure at check-out and freezes it at check-in (re-checking cycles under an advisory lock).
- `Keel.Bom.BomService`: explode (config spec `Latest` / `LatestReleased`, depth 1..10, lazy expansion by find-number path, redacted placeholders, one recursive CTE with the read filter inside the recursion), CSV export, purchasing summary, where-used (1..10 levels), compare of two iterations, working-copy read/add/update/remove/renumber/copy-from. All audited, including denials and failures.
- REST: `GET /api/v1/parts/{n}/bom`, `/bom/export.csv`, `/bom/summary`, `/bom/compare`, `/where-used`; `GET|POST /working-copy/bom`, `PUT|DELETE /working-copy/bom/{id}`, `POST /working-copy/bom/renumber`, `/copy-from`; cookie route `GET /ui/parts/{n}/bom.csv` for the UI download.
- UI: BOM section on the part page (virtualized tree grid, lazy expand/collapse, keyboard, config toggle, levels, expand/collapse all, purchasing summary, CSV link, editor for the check-out owner with inline validation), where-used page, compare page. Banner = aggregate of the part and displayed rows.
- `tests/Keel.TestSupport`: `SyntheticBomGenerator` (deterministic, seeded, bulk SQL, mixed open/ITAR/EAR markings, shared standard parts, optional released revisions) for reuse by 1.7.
- Performance gate (ADR-0003/0013) in CI.

**Tests:** see the counts in the final report; unit (rules, CSV, diff, tree model, bUnit grid, architecture rule for `Keel.Bom` queries), integration (structure, security/oracle agreement, working copy/DB guards/concurrency/audit, API, performance).

**Decisions and assumptions** (tagged `BOM-n`; not added to `assumptions.md` to avoid merge conflicts with other increments)
- BOM-1 Only parts of type Assembly own children (service and trigger).
- BOM-2 At most 5,000 direct children per parent iteration.
- BOM-3 Reference designators are unique across the parent, case-insensitively.
- BOM-4 "Released" means lifecycle state key `Released`; the highest revision (ASME order) wins; no released revision means the line is flagged unresolved, not dropped.
- BOM-5 Quantity 0 < q <= 1,000,000 with at most 6 decimals; extended quantity is the product along the path in the line's own unit (no unit conversion) and saturates at 10^18.
- BOM-6 Explosion limit 250,000 lines, where-used 100,000 rows.
- BOM-7 Cycles are prevented at add time against latest-iteration structure and re-checked at check-in under a global advisory lock; read time additionally flags circular lines.
- BOM-8 Summary defaults to leaf lines; restricted lines are never merged.
- BOM-9 Compare is single level and matches on (part, find), then part; hidden lines match on find number only.
- BOM-10 Where-used parents are revisions whose latest iteration contains the part; it expands only through parents the caller can read.
- BOM-11 The working copy is private to the owner; reading it is audited.
- BOM-12 Copy-from refuses when the source holds parts the caller cannot read.
- BOM-13 Copied rows get `gen_random_uuid()` ids (v4), not v7.
- BOM-14 Validation and conflict failures of edits are audited as `Failure`.
- BOM-15 CSV export needs the Download action (same roles as read).
- BOM-16 Lazy expansion addresses lines by find-number path.
- Finding: the CSP blocks `Virtualize` spacer styles; mitigated with a 30-line CSSOM shim (`BomTreeGrid.razor.js`), see ADR-0017. A `style-src-attr` decision is left to the CSP owner.
