# UX-3: relationships as pictures (increment 7.2)

Design: [ADR-0029](../adr/0029-visual-relationships.md). Kit and rules: [design-system.md](../ux/design-system.md).

## Delivered
- Impact map tab on PR/ECR/ECN pages (layered graph, filters, legend, detail panel, table, SVG export); BOM treemap (part, baseline items, MBOM structure) with drill-down, table, state or marking colouring with patterns; two-pane structure diff for iteration compare, baseline/unit compare, MBOM compare and EBOM/MBOM side by side; Timeline tab on part, document and MBOM pages; workflow progress map on task, workflow, and the review panels of part, change, baseline and MBOM.
- `ITimelineSource` (Keel.Core) implemented by Parts, Documents, Workflow, Change and Bom (new files); `WorkflowService.GetMapAsync`; `TimelineService` merge. No migration.

## Edits outside the new files (smallest delimited)
Keel.Core `WhereUsedEntry` (+UsedKey, Effectivity), `BomStructureImpactProvider` (fills them), `KeelUi.cs` (one registration line), `Program.cs` (`MapVisualEndpoints`), `App.razor` (stylesheet link), `_Imports.razor` (two usings), `icons.svg` (five symbols), ChangePage (Impact map tab and banner), PartPage/DocumentPage/MbomPage (Timeline tab and banner), BomSection/MbomStructure/BaselineDetailPage (treemap choice), ComparePage/DeepDiffTable/MbomComparePage/MbomSideBySidePage (table or side-by-side switch), TaskPage/WorkflowDetailPage and the four review panels (map panel).

## Assumptions
- VIS-1: Pictures use only existing service results; unchanged lines of a comparison stay a count, and unchanged ancestors appear as "Unchanged assembly" rows with their find number.
- VIS-2: Both diff panes are rows of one scrolling area, so scrolling is synchronized by construction.
- VIS-3: Treemap area is extended quantity (units not converted) or number of parts below; a restricted block counts as 1 part.
- VIS-4: "N more" counts readable items only; restricted folded items are not counted. Truncation at 500 nodes gives no count.
- VIS-5: Hidden changes, document links and MBOMs appear as neutral timeline entries (time only); hidden baselines are omitted.
- VIS-6: Lifecycle entries of a part come from successful manual transition/override audit events; releases appear through the workflow and change entries. MBOM state shows the current state of each revision only.
- VIS-7: Impact map levels shown default to 3; deeper parents fold into a "more" node that expands one level per activation.
- VIS-8: Redacted workflow map: the holder of a task who can no longer read the object sees steps and statuses, participants as "Participant", no meanings or times.
- VIS-9: Treemap budget: at most 300 top-level tiles and 400 in total; nesting one level in tiles at least 96 x 72 px.

## Measured (dev machine, Apple silicon, real Postgres)
- Layered layout of 500 nodes / 607 edges: 4 ms (budget 300 ms); impact graph of 492 nodes: build 1 ms, layout 8 ms, SVG 285 KiB in 5 ms.
- Treemap of the 50,000-line, 10-level BOM: service explosion 726 ms (existing budget 2 s), tree build 27 ms, layout 13 ms (budget 500 ms server side). Restricted user: 1,248 lines, build 1 ms.
