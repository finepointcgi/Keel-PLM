# 8.1 Quality processes (2026-10-29)

Design record: [ADR-0036](../adr/0036-quality-processes.md). Migration `0019_quality.sql`. REST: `/api/v1/quality` (OpenAPI `docs/quality/openapi.json`).

## Delivered
- Module `Keel.Quality` (schema `qual`; references Core, Security and Audit only).
- **Nonconformance reports (NCR):** against a part revision, lot and serial, with an opaque supplier reference, defect description, quantity, severity and stage; containment and
  correction actions with owners and due dates; a proposed disposition (use as is, rework, repair, scrap, return to supplier) with rationale that the material review board signs
  through the workflow engine (quality review, then every MRB member); close with a note on how it was carried out; cancel while open. No cost data.
- **CAPA:** corrective or preventive, source, priority, owner, target date; root cause analysis (whys 1-8 with evidence, six cause categories, root cause statement); action items;
  effectiveness verification plan and result; links to NCRs, changes, parts and deviations; closure only through the signed verification review.
- **Deviations and waivers:** kind, requirement reference, justification, window (two dates), optional customer approval (reference and date), scope rows (part, optional
  revision, serial range and list, lots, quantity ceiling), approval through the workflow, revoke, withdraw, automatic expiry with audit and notifications, applicability for a
  unit and date, and the part page tab "Quality".
- Three data-defined lifecycle families (view-only), review snapshots (`qual.submission`), history, links, database guards, three seeded workflow templates.
- Awareness: `AwarenessKinds`/`ObjectKinds` (ncr, capa, deviation), `IObjectAccess`, notification kinds (action assigned, action overdue, deviation ending, expired), activity catalog
  entries, watch/comments, `ITimelineSource` for parts, global search prefixes, palette, recent items, `IQualityReferenceResolver` for the supplier page.
- UI: lists, create forms, record pages with tabs (Overview, Disposition or Analysis or Scope, Actions, Verification, Links, Review, History, Activity, Comments), part tab, Home card "Quality".
- REST API and its own OpenAPI document; E2E and axe targets.

## Assumptions (FEAT-QUAL-n; not added to assumptions.md)
- FEAT-QUAL-1 "Cost-free" means no cost, price or charge field exists on any quality record.
- FEAT-QUAL-2 The supplier link of an NCR is an opaque reference text (at most 200 characters), never resolved by Quality; Sourcing points at quality records by kind and number.
- FEAT-QUAL-3 The three lifecycles are view-only families (states are bound to code and triggers); new states need a migration that publishes a new version.
- FEAT-QUAL-4 A record's markings must cover its part (NCR), its scoped parts (deviation) and the parts, reports and records it links to; links to changes are exempt.
- FEAT-QUAL-5 Dispositioned, Closed, Approved and AwaitingCustomer are reached only through an approved review; the database refuses them without an approving signature of that record.
- FEAT-QUAL-6 A CAPA goes to review only with: root cause and at least one why or cause, at least one done action and none open, a verification plan, and an effective recorded result.
- FEAT-QUAL-7 A deviation window uses UTC days, inclusive at both ends; "active" means Approved and today inside the window; a window that ends in the past cannot be submitted.
- FEAT-QUAL-8 With the customer approval required, internal approval leads to AwaitingCustomer; recording the customer's reference and date (approver role) makes it Approved; the customer declining ends it as Rejected.
- FEAT-QUAL-9 Expiry runs as a hosted job every 15 minutes by default (`Keel:Quality:MaintenanceInterval`), announces an ending window once (default 14 days, `ExpiryWarningDays`) and an overdue action once.
- FEAT-QUAL-10 Applicability: a serial or lot restriction cannot be tested without the value, so it does not match; serials are exact text or a numeric range; lots are exact text.
- FEAT-QUAL-11 Action owners must be directory users who may read the record; completing needs a note; only the owner or an administrator completes; cancelling needs the modify right and a reason.
- FEAT-QUAL-12 History rows name no linked record (only the kind), because they are shown to every reader of the record; the audit feed names links only to readers of the target.
- FEAT-QUAL-13 Dashboard counts add no marking to the Home banner; each count covers readable rows only.
- FEAT-QUAL-14 Audit details never carry title, description, rationale or analysis text (a unit test scans the module); the free-text reason of a markings change is process text, as in every module.
- FEAT-QUAL-15 Quality records are not emitted to the ERP outbox in this increment.
