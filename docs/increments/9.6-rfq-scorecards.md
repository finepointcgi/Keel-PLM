## Decisions made (RFQ-n, SCORE-n)
- RFQ-1 One request model; an RFQ is the existing request of kind `rfq`. RFQ-2 A document is pinned to the newest released revision's newest iteration at creation; "Released" is the state of the standard document lifecycle.
- RFQ-3 The request's markings must cover every document as every part. RFQ-4 Documents are sent whole or refused at read time by the outbound check on the document's own markings; the refusal answers "not found".
- RFQ-5 The price at a quantity is the tier with the largest quantity not above it; below the smallest tier there is no price. RFQ-6 No currency conversion: different currencies are never ranked.
- RFQ-7 Only a supplier's latest response counts; a later decline withdraws it. RFQ-8 A response needs a currency with any price; a validity date must not have passed. RFQ-9 After the due date the portal takes no response.
- RFQ-10 One winner per line, no split awards; a full award needs a priced quote for every line. RFQ-11 An award links or creates a DRAFT AVL entry (it still needs its approval review) and closes the request when all lines are awarded.
- RFQ-12 An award is withdrawn with a reason; its draft entry goes with it. A supplier sees only its own awards.
- SCORE-1..9 The formula (A77): weights 40 / 10 / 20 / 20 / 10, points per severity, 12 month windows, bands; unmeasured components leave the average; the trend is the same formula at 12 month ends; quality components need at least one linked record.
- SCORE-10..16 Visibility (A78): the viewer's own read rights, the supplier's release rights for its copy, publication only by a buyer who may modify the supplier and can read every linked record, a frozen snapshot with the aggregate banner of what it counts.

or feed of their own).
