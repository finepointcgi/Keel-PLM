ADR-0053. Migration 0032 (schema `rdl`). Assumptions A70 to A72 (REDLINE-n below).

  DOCX, XLSX, PDF through PdfPig 0.1.16, Apache-2.0, added to `Keel.Documents`), `RedlineReport` (HTML and PDF with banners, notices, counts, redline, change list, threads),
  `CompareModels` (comparison, sides, threads, reviews, version lists).

  root; reply shape guard; no delete; no truncate). The app role has INSERT and SELECT on reviews and SELECT, INSERT and UPDATE on annotations. No marking columns.
- **API**: `GET /api/v1/documents/compare`, `GET /api/v1/documents/compare/report?format=html|pdf`, `POST /api/v1/documents/compare/comments`,
  `POST /api/v1/documents/compare/threads/{id}/resolve|reopen`, `GET /api/v1/documents/{n}/redlines`, cookie route `/downloads/redline` (`RedlineEndpoints`).
- **UI**: `/compare` (`ComparePage`, `RedlineSegment`), `/compare/review/{id}` (`CompareReviewRedirect`), the Compare tab of a document (`CompareTab`), the redline hint of the
  Review & release panel, navigation entry "Compare documents", palette action, CSS `.rl-*` in `components.css` (tokens only), scroll module `ComparePage.razor.js`.
- **Awareness**: notification kind `redline_comment` (`NotificationKindCodes`, `NotificationKinds`), activity rows `document.redline_comment`, `document.redline_resolve`, views
  `document.compare`, `document.compare_versions`, `document.redline_export`.

## Assumptions
- REDLINE-1: reading the text of two documents needs read and download on both (A70). Without download the facts table is shown, with a sentence saying why.
- REDLINE-2: formats read as text: text, Markdown, DOCX, XLSX, PDF text layer. A PDF without a text layer is "not comparable" (no OCR).
- REDLINE-3: DOCX reads the body as shown now (tracked insertions in, tracked deletions out); headers, footers, text boxes, footnotes and comments are not read.
- REDLINE-4: XLSX lists every filled cell as "Sheet!A1: value", a formula as "=formula = cached value"; at most 500,000 cells.
- REDLINE-5: PDF text is read in the file's drawing order; each page is one paragraph, so line wrapping is not a change.
- REDLINE-6: spacing and line breaks are not changes; the display keeps the right-hand spacing.
- REDLINE-7: more than 4,000 edits in one pass shows that stretch as one replacement and the result says "coarse"; more than 600,000 tokens a side, 3,000,000 characters or 50 MB is refused.
- REDLINE-8: the comparison is never stored; every view recomputes from verified vault bytes.
- REDLINE-9: reviews and comments carry no markings; every read and write needs read and download on both documents under live attributes (A71).
- REDLINE-10: one shared review per ordered pair of iterations; the first comment creates it.
- REDLINE-11: a comment is pinned to the n-th change and its digest; a mismatch shows it as detached, never on another change.
- REDLINE-12: viewer or higher may comment (20 a minute a person, 500 a review); the thread author, or whoever can modify or sign the right-hand document, may resolve and reopen.
- REDLINE-13: the notification names the right-hand document and opens `/compare/review/{id}`, which re-authorizes; a reader of only that document sees a 404 page.
- REDLINE-14: open threads are shown on the Review & release tab and do not block release (A72).
- REDLINE-15: exports carry the aggregate banner (union of both documents, programs listed) and every distinct notice, strictest first.
- REDLINE-16: the PDF of a text over 250,000 characters shows each change with 30 words of context and says so; the HTML always shows everything.

## Not done
- No text boxes, headers, footers, footnotes or comments of DOCX; no PPTX or ODF; no OCR; no compare of non-primary files of an iteration; no three-way compare.
- Comparing a document with itself at the same version is refused (nothing to compare).
- A comment cannot be written without a changed range to pin it to (no general review comment); it can be on any change.
- Shared registries touched: `NotificationKindCodes` / `NotificationKinds`, `ActivityCatalog`, `NavModel`, `PaletteActions`, `Program.cs` (one `MapRedlineEndpoints()` line),
  `components.css` (appended block), `Keel.Documents.csproj` (PdfPig), `DocumentPage.razor` (tab), `DocumentReviewPanel.razor` (hint), manual chapter 5, ADR index, assumptions.
  No new module (the feature lives in `Keel.Documents`), so `ArchitectureTests` module lists and `update-openapi.sh` are unchanged (the routes are not part of the integration API).
