## Assumptions (FEAT-PRC-n)
- **FEAT-PRC-1** One plan per MBOM; plant and part come from the MBOM. A plan needs a Released MBOM revision to exist and is created In work with a checked-out first iteration.
- **FEAT-PRC-2** Read needs plan, MBOM and part. The plan filter is SQL; MBOM readability is checked per row through the MBOM module, so a list is read in at most 500 candidate plans and counted after that check.
- **FEAT-PRC-3** Plan markings must cover the MBOM's and the part's (the minimal cover is offered and needs confirmation). Changing a plan's markings after creation is not built.
- **FEAT-PRC-4** Time is plain data in minutes: setup per run, run per unit; no costing, scheduling or capacity. Work center is a code of the plant's admin data.
- **FEAT-PRC-5** Conditions on routings and operations are opaque text up to 500 characters, shown as written and signed with the content. They are not parsed or evaluated; the variants condition language
  is not imported (no module reference), so text is the contract.
- **FEAT-PRC-6** Assignments identify a line by find number and alternate; quantity null means the whole line. A phantom line is not consumed. Over-assignment and assignments to missing lines are errors; unassigned lines are warnings.
- **FEAT-PRC-7** Stale means the MBOM's latest Released revision (or its digest) differs from the binding; stale plans are flagged in lists, on the page, on the MBOM tab and in findings, never changed. Release of a stale plan is refused.
- **FEAT-PRC-8** The working copy is the open iteration, private to its owner (others see the last checked-in iteration). Undo is not possible on the first iteration of a revision (check it in instead).
- **FEAT-PRC-9** A new revision of a Released plan starts from the released content bound to the revision released now; the earlier Released revision stays in force until the new one is released, then becomes Obsolete.
- **FEAT-PRC-10** Callouts: caution and warning (safety), quality check, note; each shown with its word and a symbol. Attachments need a caption; images are shown inline through the viewer content route, other files as links.
- **FEAT-PRC-11** Attachments are checked at save (viewable by the editor, exists, not quarantined) and at view (per reader). A quarantined or missing attachment is an error finding and blocks release. An attachment the editor cannot view is returned blank and restored on save.
- **FEAT-PRC-12** Tools are marked. A retired tool cannot be added; one already used shows an error until removed. A gauge or tool that needs calibration and is overdue (or never calibrated) is an error finding (counts only, no names) and blocks submission.
- **FEAT-PRC-13** Calibration notices at 30, 14, 7 days and overdue (once per tool, stage, due date) to watchers of the tool; only a reference is stored, never a certificate. Calibration history is append-only.
- **FEAT-PRC-14** Work centers are retired, not deleted. Plants are those of the MBOM module.
- **FEAT-PRC-15** The shop floor view shows a Released (or Obsolete, by revision) plan only, one routing at a time; the screen banner is the shell's, the printed banner is repeated by the stylesheet; neither is optional.
- **FEAT-PRC-16** Audit details carry numbers, codes and counts only: step text, names and notes are never written to the audit log.
- **FEAT-PRC-17** The MBOM port writes no audit events (see ADR-0042); the plan service audits denials of the MBOM check on its own reads.

## Not built / not verified
- Plan markings change, ERP event for plan release, plan-wide notifications when the MBOM revision changes (staleness is computed, not pushed), where-used of components or tools beyond the tool page.
- A routing condition editor with validation against the variants language; drag and drop ordering; bulk import of routings; step images uploaded directly (they come from documents of the vault).
- Attachments through a real vault in the integration tests (the plan service is tested with no file source; the documents-based path is covered by unit and E2E expectations only).
